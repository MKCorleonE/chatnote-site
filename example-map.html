    Help me understand Transformers step by step. Start with Q, K, and V.

    助手

    Three representations, three roles

    Each token’s input vector is multiplied by three separate learned projection matrices to produce Query (Q), Key (K), and Value (V). Q describes what a position is looking for, K provides features to match against, and V carries the information to aggregate.

    Think of Q as a search request, K as an index label, and V as the information behind that label. All three are vectors learned by the model.

    From scores to attention weights

    Attention computes the dot products of Q with each K, divides by √dₖ, and applies softmax to obtain weights. A weighted sum of V produces a new representation.

    Attention(Q,K,V)=softmax(QKTdk)V\mathrm{Attention}(Q,K,V)=\mathrm{softmax}\left(\frac{QK^T}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)V

      Why do Q and K use different projection matrices?

      助手

      Q and K serve different roles: one expresses what I need and the other what I can match. Separate projections let the model learn these representations independently.

      Matching relationships do not have to be symmetric. A verb may seek information from its subject, while the subject may not attend to the verb equally.
